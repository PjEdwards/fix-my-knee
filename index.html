Day 1: Lower Body Strength & Tracking

Build muscular shock absorbers to shield the joints when carrying your golf bag.

Tuesday Option

10 Mins

Set to a low resistance with a smooth, spinning cadence. Serves to pump synovial fluid ("joint lubricant") through your knee structures prior to weighting.

3 Sets x 30s

Lean flat back against the wall holding a light weight (10–15 lbs) close to chest. Lower down to a shallow 45° to 60° knee bend. Form Note: Avoid dropping to a full 90° angle if it causes joint pinch or grinding.

3 Sets x 10/leg

Utilize your flat gym bench or a 6-inch step. Stand holding light dumbbells. Step up deliberately, ensuring your tracking knee stays directly centered over your toes. Crucial Constraint: Do not let the knee cave inward at any point during descent.

3 Sets x 15/side

Loop a resistance band immediately above the knees. Lie on your side and elevate your top knee against the tension. Targets the outer glutes to mitigate inward rotational instability on rolling golf terrain.

Desk-Side Straight Leg Raises 3 Sets x 12/leg

Sit upright on your bench. Fully extend your affected leg parallel to the ground and lock out the knee, squeezing the quadriceps intensely for 5 full seconds. Loop a light band around your ankles for progressive loading.

Weighted Glute Bridges 3 Sets x 12 Reps

Lie flat on your back with knees bent, feet firmly planted. Position a comfortable dumbbell weight across your pelvic crest. Drive down through your heels to raise your hips. Offloads severe mechanical work from the lower quad tendons by optimizing posterior chain support.

Bird-Dogs 3 Sets x 10/side

Adopt a quadruped stance on all fours. Uniformly extend your opposite arm and leg perfectly straight. Stabilizes cross-body patterns to protect your lower back from the asymmetrical rotational loading forces inherent in a golf swing.

⛳ Pre-Round Parking Lot Preparation

Execute this non-weight-bearing sequence immediately before teeing off to secure early stabilization.

1️⃣

Supported Standing Leg Swings

Hold onto your car door or golf cart. Gently swing your leg forward and backward 15 times to wake up the hamstrings and hip flexors without high-impact compression.

2️⃣

Mini-Band Lateral Walks (Optional Activation)

If you keep a light exercise loop in your golf bag, slide it above your ankles and perform 10 micro-steps to each side. This locks in glute medius tracking so your knee won't alter your gait on early holes.

3️⃣

Club-Assisted Torso Twists

Place a long iron across your shoulders behind your neck. Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart, and smoothly rotate your torso left to right. Focus on keeping your hips stable to prevent premature twisting force inside the knee joints.
